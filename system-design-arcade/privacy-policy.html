Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Overview

System Design Arcade is a mobile app that teaches system design concepts through interactive, arcade-style games. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

The short version: We collect your email and game progress if you sign in, and we measure how the app is used so we can improve it. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't build advertising profiles.

What Data We Collect

If you play as a Guest:

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any other information that identifies you personally.
  • Game progress is stored only on your device (locally, via AsyncStorage).
  • We do collect anonymous usage analytics — see Usage Analytics below. These are tied to a randomly generated device identifier, not to you.

If you sign in (Google or Apple):

  • Email address — provided by your OAuth provider. Used to identify your account, and associated with your usage analytics.
  • Display name — if provided by your OAuth provider. You can change it in Settings.
  • Avatar — an emoji you pick in the app. If your Google or Apple account supplies a profile picture, we may store the link to it. We never upload or host photos from your device.
  • Game progress — scores, stars, streaks, and level completion data, synced to the cloud so you can access it across devices.
  • Country — a two-letter country code derived from your device's region setting, used for the country leaderboard.
  • Friends — if you add friends via a friend code, we store that connection.
  • Event participation — if you register for a timed event, we store your registration, when you registered, and your score at the event's start.

We do not collect your location (beyond the country code above), contacts, photos, microphone input, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

Usage Analytics

We use PostHog for product analytics, so we can see which parts of the app work and which don't. This applies to both Guest and signed-in users.

What we measure:

  • Screens you open and the order you move between them.
  • Games and levels started, completed, or abandoned; scores and streaks.
  • Whether a challenge answer was correct, and what type of challenge it was. We do not record the content of your answers in analytics — only correct/incorrect and the challenge type. The optional AI Design Coach is the one exception; see below.
  • Which features you tap (share, leaderboard, hints, daily challenge, theme settings, and similar).
  • App version and build, operating system and version, device type, and language.
  • Your IP address, which PostHog receives with each event and uses to derive an approximate region.

If you are signed in, these events are linked to your account and email. If you are a Guest, they are linked only to a random device identifier.

We do not use session recording, screen recording, or heatmaps — these are switched off. We do not use analytics for advertising, and we do not share or sell this data.

Analytics events are retained for 12 months. There is currently no in-app switch to turn usage analytics off — email us if you would like your analytics data deleted.

Error and Diagnostic Reports

When the app hits an error, a diagnostic report is sent to PostHog so we can fix it. A report contains the error type (for example TypeError), the error message, and a stack trace showing where in our code it happened. It also carries the same app version, device and session information as our other analytics events, and — if you are signed in — is associated with your account.

Error messages can occasionally contain incidental data such as an account identifier or a file path. We make a best effort to keep personal data out of these reports and are actively improving how they are filtered before they leave your device.

We do not collect crash reports from the operating system itself.

AI Design Coach (optional, off by default)

Some architecture challenges offer optional AI feedback. This is switched off unless you turn it on, and limited to a few uses per day.

If you enable it, the following is sent to our AI coaching service — a server we operate, which passes the request to a third-party language model — each time you ask for feedback:

  • The architecture you built, including any component names you typed.
  • The challenge brief and how your answer scored against it.
  • Your account identifier and a token proving you are signed in.

Nothing is sent to this service unless you have switched the AI coach on. You can turn it off again at any time in Settings.

Leaderboards and Social Features

System Design Arcade has public leaderboards. If you are signed in and appear on one, the following is publicly visible — including to people who are not signed in:

  • Your display name
  • Your total score and rank
  • Your country, on the country leaderboard

Your email address is never shown on a leaderboard.

You can remove yourself from public leaderboards at any time: Settings → Privacy → "Show me on leaderboard". You will still appear on the friends leaderboard of people who have added you as a friend. Guests do not appear on any leaderboard.

Notifications

If you turn on study reminders, the app schedules notifications locally on your device. We do not use a push notification service and we do not collect a push token.

How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account and sync your game progress across devices.
  • To rank you on leaderboards, if you have not opted out.
  • To understand how the app is used, so we can improve the content and fix what's broken.
  • To diagnose and fix errors and crashes.

We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for any purpose other than improving this app.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud database for progress sync. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • PostHog — product analytics and error diagnostics (US region). PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Google OAuth — for "Sign in with Google". Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign In — for "Sign in with Apple". Apple Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts our optional AI Design Coach service. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — app build and update infrastructure. Expo Privacy Policy

We do not use any advertising networks, ad SDKs, or advertising identifiers.

Data Storage & Security

  • Guest game progress is stored locally on your device only.
  • Signed-in user data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS) with row-level security enabled.
  • Analytics and diagnostic events are stored by PostHog in the United States.
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.
  • All communication with our servers uses HTTPS encryption.

Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account exists.

If you delete your account, your profile, game progress, statistics and event registrations are permanently deleted from our database, and your login is removed. Two things deliberately survive:

  • A minimal deletion record — your user ID, email address, IP address, browser/app user agent, and the time of deletion. We keep this so we can answer disputes about whether and when an account was deleted.
  • Analytics and diagnostic events already recorded, which expire on their normal 12-month schedule. Email us if you want them removed sooner.

Analytics events are retained for 12 months from when they were recorded. The user profile PostHog builds from them persists until you ask us to remove it.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Play without an account — Guest mode collects no personally identifying information.
  • Delete your account in the app — Settings → Data → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated server-side data.
  • Request data deletion by email — if you'd rather not use the in-app flow, email us and we will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
  • Opt out of public leaderboards — Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn off the AI Design Coach — Settings. It is off by default.
  • Sign out — at any time from Settings within the app.
