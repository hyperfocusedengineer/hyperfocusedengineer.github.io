The session loop

Most focus apps time you. Brevn also asks, briefly, how it went, and over a few weeks turns those answers into a picture of when your focus is at its best.

Start. One tap, from the app, a widget, Siri, the Action Button or your Apple Watch. Nothing to set up first.
Focus. The timer keeps going. A stray thought? Park it in a line and carry on; it waits for you at the end.
Two taps. How absorbed were you, and did you get done what you meant to? Both optional.
Your patterns. After a few sessions: the hours, lengths and breaks that rate highest for you, with how sure Brevn is.

What's inside

Sharp hours
Which time of day, session length and break rhythm rate highest in your own sessions. When the data is thin or the gap is small, Brevn says so.
The Lab
Test a hunch properly. Brevn assigns each day a condition at random (music or silence, coffee or not) and tells you whether the difference is real.
Park a thought
Write down the stray thought without stopping the timer, from the app, the Lock Screen or Siri. Go over them after the session.
Where you left off
One line at the end of a session. Brevn hands it back the next time you start on the same thing.
Start from anywhere
Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center, the Action Button, Apple Watch, and a menu bar app for the Mac.
Sleep, caffeine, heart rate
Log a coffee with a tap. If you choose, Brevn also reads sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from Apple Health and sets them beside your sessions.

Calm by design

The same constraints apply to every feature, every screen, every notification.

  • No streaks. A missed day isn't a failure.
  • No exclamation marks. No celebrations. A good session is its own reward.
  • Nothing gates the start. No test, no setup, no account needed.
  • Private by default. Your sessions stay on your phone unless you sign in to keep them in your account. No ads, no selling data.
  • Honest findings. Compared only with yourself. When the sample is too small, Brevn says so.
