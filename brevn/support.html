Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Every feature works without one, and your sessions stay on your phone. Sign in only if you want your sessions kept in your account and shared between your devices.

Why don't I see my sharp hours yet?
Brevn needs at least six answered sessions, with at least three in each of two different times of day, before it shows a pattern. When the data is thin or the difference is small, it says so rather than guess.

How do I start a session without opening the app?
Add the Brevn widget, ask Siri to "Start a focus session in Brevn", or set it on the Action Button or in Control Center. Profile → Start from anywhere walks through each one.

How do I restore Brevn Pro on a new phone?
In Profile, under Brevn Pro, tap See plans, then Restore at the bottom, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. To cancel a subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Are my Health readings uploaded?
Only if you turn on "Health readings in my account" in Profile. Turning it off deletes them from your account.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. Your account and everything stored in it are deleted straight away (for Sign in with Apple, you confirm with Apple first). Sessions on your phone stay on your phone; clear them with Profile → Clear all sessions.
