Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open HyperFit → gear icon (top-right of Home) → Profile → Delete Account. Two-step confirmation, then everything is removed server-side.

I forgot my password.
HyperFit uses Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In — there's no HyperFit password to forget. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you originally signed in with.

My data isn't syncing across devices.
Make sure you're signed in with the same account on both devices. Pull-to-refresh on the Home tab forces a re-fetch. If it persists, email us with the device names and we'll dig in.

Can I export my workout history?
Not yet — we're working on a CSV export. Email us if this is blocking you and we'll prioritize it.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email hyperfocusedengineer@gmail.com with the device, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Screenshots help.

Can I join the beta?
Yes — TestFlight invite. Free, no account required to install.
