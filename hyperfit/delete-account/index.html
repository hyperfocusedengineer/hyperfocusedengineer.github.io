Account deletion

Delete your HyperFit account

HyperFit, by Hyperfocused Engineer, on Android and iPhone. You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time: in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

In the app
  1. Open HyperFit and tap your initials at the top right of any tab to open Profile.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what gets deleted, then tap I understand · continue.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Your account is deleted at once, and HyperFit signs you out and clears its copy on the phone.

Without the app

Email hyperfocusedengineer@gmail.com with the subject Delete my HyperFit account, from the email address you sign in with (for Sign in with Google, your Google account's address). If you write from another address, we reply to that sign-in address to check it's you before deleting anything.

Email a deletion request

We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted
  • Your sign-in identity: email, name and account ID.
  • Every workout session and set, including cardio, stretch and follow-along sessions and imported workouts.
  • Your plans (including any you shared or published), the plans and routines you follow, custom exercises and exercise notes.
  • Readiness check-ins, fitness check results and body-weight entries.
  • Meals, water logs, nutrition targets and the foods you saved.
  • Your training-buddy link, invites and nudges.
  • Your profile answers and settings, including sex if you gave it.
  • Usage events and crash reports tied to your account in our analytics service (PostHog).
What is kept, and for how long
  • Nothing that identifies you is kept on purpose. Usage events and crash reports tied to your account are deleted from PostHog when your account is deleted (processing can take up to 30 days).
  • Encrypted backups made for disaster recovery are kept for the backup retention period of our database provider (typically up to 7 days on our plan). A deleted account disappears from them as they expire, and they are never used to restore it.
  • Sign-in audit records held by our database provider, which include your email and account ID, are kept only for the provider's log retention period.
  • Deletion can't be undone. You can sign up again with the same email, but you start with no data.

More on what HyperFit stores and why: privacy policy. Questions: hyperfocusedengineer@gmail.com.
