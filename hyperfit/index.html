What's inside

Strength & nutrition tracking, designed like Strava and Robinhood had a kid.

Anthracite base, strava-orange for the active set, robinhood-green when your session volume beats the last one. Mono numerics with tabular figures so every readout lines up. Every screen is built around one question: did the work happen, yes or no?

— 01
Anatomy-aware muscle heatmap
Recruitment-weighted volume per muscle, rendered onto a front/back silhouette. Toggle between volume mode and a recovery-decay model that shows what's actually fresh today.
— 02
Plan creator + active-plan rail
Build a multi-day split, assign sessions to days of the week, and the app schedules from there. One plan active at a time — no clutter.
— 03
Shareable workout cards
Every finished session generates a 1080×1350 card with your silhouette, top muscles, and PR badges — ready for stories. Re-share past sessions from history too.
— 04
PR detection
Heaviest weight, rep, estimated-1RM and volume records, judged only against your own history. The PERSONAL RECORDS card and the share card show it actually happened.
— 05
Last time and today’s target
Set fields show last time’s numbers and today’s target as faded placeholders. LOG SET or USE fills them in with one tap, so you only type when you beat it.
— 06
Editable session history
Calendar deep-links into a session detail view with grouped sets per exercise, duration, total volume, and a share button that works months later. Tap the pencil to fix a typo’d weight or rep without re-logging.
— 07
Nutrition tracking
Per-day macro pills, meal entries with slot (breakfast / lunch / dinner / snack) and a free-text label like “Pre-workout.” Cheat-day toggle that doesn’t pretend the meal didn’t happen. The This Week card folds nutrition + lifting into one signal.
— 08
Water intake, the way you actually drink
One-tap CUP or BOTTLE presets plus + for any amount, in ml or fl oz. Stored in ml, displayed in ml or oz per your setting. Water is the rare metric where more is unambiguously better, so it gets robinhood-green every time you log a tap.
— 09
Social shareable plans & cards
Flip a plan to shareable to mint a code; anyone with it lands on a public preview page with the muscle silhouette already lit up by intensity. Workout cards drop straight into Instagram / Stories / iMessage at 1080×1350. Rotate or revoke a share code anytime.
— 10
Onboarding that respects your time
Four screens: name + units, goal (BUILD / CUT / MAINTAIN / JUST TRACK), the gear you have, and a starter plan that fits it. Under a minute before your first set.
Shipped since the beta

What shipped, and what’s next.

Every feature was tested the same way: in a real workout, by the person who built it.

— NEW
Follow-along routines
Mobility, core, a bodyweight circuit and walk/run intervals, with a voice coach that counts reps and gives form cues. Easier, harder, or a different pace in one tap.
— NEW
Apple Watch & widgets
Log sets and start rest from the wrist. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show today’s session and your week. A Live Activity counts down rest on the lock screen.
— NEW
Siri shortcuts
“Start Core 10 in HyperFit”, “Log water in HyperFit”, “Log my weight in HyperFit”. Water, weight and cardio logs work even with the app closed.
— NEW
Smarter sets
Next-set targets from last session, suggested rest by lift, warm-up ramps and plate math, drop sets, supersets, and a short version of the session when you’re short on time.
— NEW
Fitness check & cardio
Nine self-tests (pull-ups, dead hang, sit-rise…) judged against your own last result. Log runs, rides and swims. Steps and cardio from Apple Health, on the phone only.
— NEW
Describe or photograph a meal
Type “2 eggs and a cup of rice” and get USDA numbers, or point the camera at a nutrition label. It’s read on the device, and the photo is never uploaded.
— NEW
Your foods & quick log
Foods you log are remembered and scale to the amount you type. Your usual breakfast is one tap.
— NEXT
Android
The codebase is already cross-platform. What’s left is store assets, signing and a real device pass.
