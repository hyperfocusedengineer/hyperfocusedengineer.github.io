HFE Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

HFE Publisher is a private tool that the owner of a small group of YouTube channels runs on their own computer. It publishes and manages videos on the owner's own YouTube channels only. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, HFE Publisher requests these YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's own videos.
  • Manage the channel (youtube.force-ssl): to set video titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists, privacy and schedules; to set channel branding such as the description, keywords and banner; to post comments on the owner's own videos (for example, a pinned question for viewers); and to read the owner's own video and channel details, such as upload status and basic statistics.
  • Read analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the owner's own channel statistics, such as views and watch time.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own channels, to show the owner their own statistics, and to use those statistics to improve the owner's future videos.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by that user. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Video details and channel statistics retrieved from YouTube are saved in the owner's project files on their computer, and may be copied to the owner's own private Google Cloud (Firebase) database so the owner can view them from other devices. No one else has access to it.
  • HFE Publisher does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.
  • HFE Publisher never deletes videos.

Retention and deletion

Stored statistics are refreshed regularly and kept only as long as the owner needs them to manage the channels. If access is revoked, the stored YouTube data (the local project files and the owner's private database copy) is deleted within 30 days. To request deletion at any time, email the contact below; requests are completed within 7 days.

YouTube and Google

HFE Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. HFE Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke HFE Publisher's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the local token file removes the stored sign-in from the computer; to revoke the grant itself, use the permissions page.
